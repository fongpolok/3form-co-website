How to request indexing
  1. Click Inspect in Search Console. Sign in with the account that owns 3formhk.com if asked.
  2. Wait about 10 seconds. A new page shows “URL is not on Google”. That is normal.
  3. Click Request indexing and wait 1–2 minutes for Google's live test.
  4. When it says Indexing requested, click Got it, come back here and tick the box.
  5. If the link opens the wrong screen, click Copy URL and paste it into the search bar at the top of Search Console instead.
